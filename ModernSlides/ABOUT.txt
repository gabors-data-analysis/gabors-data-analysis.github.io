Only index.html and style-gallery.html are hosted here. The PHP "Publish" helpers are not
included (GitHub Pages cannot run PHP).

Adding a deck:
  1. Build it at https://gabors-data-analysis.com/ModernSlides/ (e = edit/present, s = speaker view).
  2. Click Download -> <Name>.json (plus any pasted images, as a zip).
  3. Put the files in ModernSlides/<Name>/<Name>.json in this repo and push.
  4. Open at https://gabors-data-analysis.com/ModernSlides/?deck=<Name>
