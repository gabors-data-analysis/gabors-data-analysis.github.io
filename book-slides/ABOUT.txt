book-slides — browser slides about the textbook, built with ModernSlides by Kevin A. Bryan

Local changes in index.html, all marked "gabors-data-analysis.com patch":
  1. the page opens the DA-Textbook deck when no ?deck= is given;
  2. a "viridis" theme (CSS at the end of #app-styles, plus an entry in THEME_IDS), with
     per-slide colours keyed on DA-Textbook slide ids (title, quotes, part-1..4, second-edition).
Everything else is unmodified. Keep these when updating index.html from upstream.
The PHP "Publish" helpers are not included (GitHub Pages cannot run PHP).

Decks:
  DA-Textbook/DA-Textbook.json  -> https://gabors-data-analysis.com/book-slides/
  <Name>/<Name>.json            -> https://gabors-data-analysis.com/book-slides/?deck=<Name>

Editing: open the page, press e (edit), Download the .json, put it back in its folder, push.
